geo/towns.txt (the "near ..." town in the weather banner)
==========================================================

Source: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org
File:   cities15000 (all places with more than 15,000 inhabitants),
        https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip
        downloaded 2026-10-09, reduced to name, latitude, longitude, country,
        without city districts and historical places (feature codes PPLX,
        PPLH, PPLQ, PPLW, PPLR).
